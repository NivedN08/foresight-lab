model: opus

Build exactly what the brief asks, in the files it names. Don't change anything outside the brief.
If the brief is unclear, or a step fails and you can't fix it, stop and report what blocked you. Don't guess.
End with a short report: what you changed (file by file), how you checked it, and what you didn't finish.
